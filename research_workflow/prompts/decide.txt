Use the family's result trajectory to choose NEW_DIRECTION, OPTIMIZE, TUNE, or SUBMIT, and give an evidence-based reason. If a failed history item has a BRAIN compilation or unit error in its error field, use that concrete feedback to correct the expression; do not repeat the invalid form. A WARNING during simulation is not a final failure.
